# Animation validation procedure

Scope: T001–T015; local FR-001–010 and PRD AC-066–071. Follow [quickstart A–G](../quickstart.md) for the complete feature acceptance procedures; this record defines the comparison conditions before asset production.

## Candidate review (A)

Compare the preserved current GLB, KayKit and Universal side by side on each actual character. Use the same orthographic camera, lighting, ground, display scale 1.25, elapsed time and facing. Review Idle, Move and Attack at normal speed and at paused samples 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75 and 1. Repeat facing -1 and +1. Capture the same viewport (844×390 landscape) and full-size views when examining attachments. All views own their skeleton and sampling time. Pause freezes time; changing sample or character is explicit.

Inspect upper/lower limb bending, stance/foot sliding, silhouette, jackets, horns, mane, Crow's feather groups and Plates' entire rigid plate/face. Compare actual imported bone transforms, not just animation names. Record each source's exact file/clip/hash, rig revision, mapping, selected sample intervals, root exclusion, export size, adaptation limits and missing states. Retain failed trials. Do not substitute a mannequin or claim a trial subset covers production semantics.

Each character's source decision needs actual owner feedback on the three matched views. Until supplied, label selection pending and retain a proposed source/custom recommendation. No full production is authorized by a successful import alone.

## Fighter and partner matrix (B–C)

For each of Cow, Crow, Lion and Plates in both facings, review preview, Idle, forward/backward/depth/diagonal Move, start/stop/turn/blocked travel, Light 1–3, Heavy, directed Dodge, Special, Hurt and KnockedOut. Review all twelve ordered distinct duos for ordinary follow, pursuit, catch-up, support contact, reaction, knockout, solo continuation and fresh Retry. Use the exact pair list in quickstart C. Include crowded normal gameplay in all four rooms, muted and without shake. Ten Home/retry cycles must return owned resources to baseline.

## Lifecycle and delivery (D–F)

Use quickstart D–F unchanged: interrupt every action/terminal fall, clear input, require explicit Resume, keep results/Retry/Home immediate, and retain still previews under reduced motion. Validate missing scene/profile/clip and stale load generation, complete cache truth, offline credits, safe between-run updates and nonblocking audio/storage failures. Run focused red/green tests for each change and the full suite before a commit. Preserve deterministic fixtures and historical feature 010 fingerprints.

## Evidence fields and acceptance dependencies (G)

Every outcome records source/build hashes, date/reviewer, character/role/duo, action/facing, room/camera, viewport/settings, result and artifact paths (data-model ValidationEvidence). Test logs record command, exit status and summary. Phone records additionally identify actual device, OS/browser, full-run frame intervals, active busiest-encounter windows, stalls and sustained-play behavior; use the intended soundtrack. Require 60 fps target and 30 fps minimum as defined by quickstart G.

Five-player records separately capture counterbalanced preference, Light/Heavy recognition, each Special, companion inactivity, responsiveness/readability ratings and Lion/Plates style judgments. Apply each threshold separately. Device and participant access is not established in this session; all such checks remain pending until actual records exist. Desktop Chromium/WebKit captures are supplementary. Owner review, physical devices and five participants are dependencies, never assumed passes.
