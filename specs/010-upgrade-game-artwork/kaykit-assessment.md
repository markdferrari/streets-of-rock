# KayKit assessment — 2026-10-14

The owner liked the existing concept images and requested evaluation of all local KayKit directories, particularly their character animations.

[Full assessment and evidence](../../../kaykit-assessment/assessment.md) inspect eight packs / 798 glTF files and verify 15 selected animation clips by actual Godot pose sampling.

Recommended motion source: **KayKit Character Animations 1.1**, Medium for Grunt/Zoner and potentially Large for Enforcer/Liam. Medium includes a real Throw and unarmed punch; Large includes unarmed punch/smash and two-handed slam. Reuse idle/movement/hit/death. Charge, dedicated stun and rope/impact presentation still need adaptation, and existing hero rigs remain unchanged.

Dungeon bottles/shelves/stools and RPG Tools rope bundles are useful scenery/prop candidates. The full report covers Adventurers, Skeletons, Platformer, Halloween and Fantasy Weapons, source licenses, duplicate libraries, rig compatibility, hips displacement and packaging limits. This is an inspected recommendation, not completed production art or integration.
