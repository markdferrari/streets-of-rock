The [CC0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) describes permission to copy, modify and distribute, including commercially, without permission. The game still needs the exact downloaded asset's provenance and license evidence. Do not assume a paid bundle or another pack shares the same terms merely because it has the same creator.

Selection procedure for planning:

1. Inspect the actual free download and retain its license and source/version record.
2. Compare candidates beside Cow, Crow, Lion and Plates at normal gameplay scale; assess setting/role recognition and readable silhouettes.
3. List actual available enemy actions and missing states. An animation count alone does not establish coverage.
4. Test a representative furnished room and mixed enemy encounter against the inherited mobile and content budgets before producing every asset.
5. Reuse suitable candidates; record why alternatives were rejected. Create or adapt missing venue details and actions as original work. Do not introduce purchases or select a mismatched pack solely because it is free.

These are leads for the feature's planning phase, not a completed asset inventory or runtime validation record.
